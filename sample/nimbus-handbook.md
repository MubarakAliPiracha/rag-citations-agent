# Nimbus Coffee Co. — Employee Handbook

## About Nimbus Coffee Co.
Nimbus Coffee Co. was founded in 2019 in Portland, Oregon. We roast small-batch,
ethically sourced coffee and operate twelve cafés across the Pacific Northwest.
Our mission is to serve exceptional coffee while treating every employee and
grower fairly. The company is privately held and employs roughly 240 people.

## Working Hours and Remote Work
Nimbus runs a hybrid schedule. Corporate and support staff work three days
remotely and two days in the office each week. Core collaboration hours are
10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Pacific Time, during which everyone should be reachable.
Café staff follow shift schedules published two weeks in advance. Employees may
request a fully remote arrangement, which requires manager and People-team
approval and is reviewed every six months.

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly.
In addition, Nimbus observes 10 paid company holidays. Up to 5 unused vacation
days may roll over into the following calendar year; anything beyond 5 days is
forfeited. Sick leave is separate and uncapped, provided it is used in good faith.

## Parental Leave
Nimbus offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all new parents,
regardless of gender, and this may be taken any time within the first year after
a birth or adoption. Leave can be split into two blocks with manager approval.

## Coffee, Perks, and Reimbursements
Every employee receives a monthly coffee allowance of $50 to spend at any Nimbus
café. Staff also get a 30% discount on retail bags of beans and merchandise.
Approved business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submission through
the Expensify portal. Nimbus contributes up to $1,200 per year toward
professional development, such as courses, conferences, or certifications.

## Equipment
New corporate hires receive a 14-inch MacBook Pro, which is refreshed every three
years. Café employees are issued branded aprons and non-slip footwear. All
equipment remains the property of Nimbus Coffee Co. and must be returned when
employment ends.

## Code of Conduct
Nimbus expects honesty, respect, and inclusion from everyone. Harassment or
discrimination of any kind is grounds for termination. Employees must not share
confidential roasting recipes or supplier contracts outside the company. Concerns
can be reported anonymously through the People team's confidential inbox.
